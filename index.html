“The Voice of a New Generation”

Biography

Early Life

Ado became interested in music in 2014 after discovering Nico Nico, a Japanese video-sharing platform. Inspired by singers who performed without showing their faces, she began uploading her own singing videos in 2017.

Career

Ado made her major-label debut in 2020 with Usseewa, which topped major Japanese music charts. In 2022, she released her first studio album, Kyogen.

Her international recognition grew when she sang the songs performed by Uta in ONE PIECE FILM RED. She later expanded her global presence with the Wish world tour in 2024 and the Hibana tour in 2025.
